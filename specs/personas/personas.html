Family parent

I am a father/mother. I can be any age, from any social background. As a parent, my focus is mainly on being able to easily organize my family life as well as my own life.

In particular, I want to combine family life with my work and my personal hobbies easily. I want it to be simple for me to take personal time, and to be able to give the same back to the other parent. I want organizing everyday life (groceries, holidays, childminder, housework, etc.) to be simple.

Child

I am a child. Depending on my age, I want to take part in family life more or less actively. When I am very young, I have no access to the family's organization; my role is to learn and have fun. As I grow up, I want to be able to take part by organizing myself for school and for holidays.

Grandparents

I am a grandparent. I want to help my children care for their own children. I want it to be simple for me to know whether they need help and when. And I want to be able to easily tell them whether I am available to come and help.

Extended family (uncles, aunts, …)

As a member of the family (parent, grandparent, etc.), I want an easy way to share things with my family, such as photos, and I also want to be able to organize holidays with some specific members of my family.
